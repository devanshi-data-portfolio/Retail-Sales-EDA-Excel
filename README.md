# Retail Sales & Customer Behavior – Exploratory Data Analysis (Excel)

## About the Project
This project explores retail transaction data to better understand how sales perform
across product categories, time periods, and customer segments.

The goal was not to build complex models, but to practice **structured exploratory data analysis (EDA)**
using Excel — asking the right business questions, organizing the data properly,
and extracting meaningful insights using PivotTables and charts.

---

## What I Worked With
- ~1000 retail transactions  
- Time period: 2023 (with limited data for January 2024)  
- Product categories: Electronics, Clothing, Beauty  
- Customer information: Age and Gender  

---

## What I Tried to Answer
During the analysis, I focused on questions such as:
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- How do sales change month to month?
- Do different categories behave differently over time?
- Which customer age groups and genders contribute most to sales?
- Are some categories driven by frequent purchases while others rely on high-value orders?

---

## Key Takeaways
Some of the main insights from the analysis include:
- Electronics generates the highest total revenue, mainly due to higher-value purchases.
- Beauty records the highest number of transactions, making it more volume-driven.
- Sales show noticeable variation across months, indicating seasonal patterns.
- Customers aged 26–45 contribute the largest share of overall revenue.
- Different product categories are driven by different customer segments and buying behaviors.
